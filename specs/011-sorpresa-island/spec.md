# 011 Sorpresa Island

## Objective

Make Sorpresa a playable island after Confianza, following the current EMOPLAY island pattern.

## Scope

- Add Sorpresa to the playable route after Confianza.
- Provide a six-page story about body signals, information, different reactions, and approaching change at a safe pace.
- Add a character reference, six story illustrations, final reflection, story reward, achievements, challenges, and related rewards.
- Make Afectividad the next map phase without developing its content.

## Educational Tone

- Surprise may feel pleasant, confusing, or uncomfortable.
- Different reactions to the same event are valid.
- The player can pause, ask what changed, observe first, seek support, and respect personal limits.
- No forced participation, frightening reveal, unsafe secret, or intimidating scene.

## Out Of Scope

- No story, challenges, illustrations, or gameplay for Afectividad.
- No changes to completed island content.
- No mobile polish phase or exhaustive manual walkthrough.
- No changes to the saved final-island concept for Amor.

## Acceptance

- Completing Confianza unlocks Sorpresa.
- Sorpresa can be opened, read, completed, and followed by its retos.
- Completing Sorpresa unlocks Afectividad as the next prepared map phase.
- The six story images load from project assets.
- Build and available technical checks pass.

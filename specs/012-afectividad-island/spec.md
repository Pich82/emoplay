# 012 Afectividad Island

## Objective

Make Afectividad a playable island after Sorpresa, following the current EMOPLAY island pattern.

## Scope

- Add Afectividad to the playable route after Sorpresa.
- Provide a six-page story about different caring gestures, asking before approaching, consent, personal space, and respectful alternatives.
- Add a character reference, six story illustrations, final reflection, story reward, achievements, challenges, and related rewards.
- Make Asco the next map phase without developing its content.

## Educational Tone

- Afectividad includes friendship, family care, self-respect, and safe relationships; it is not romantic content.
- A caring gesture is always optional and may take many forms.
- The player can ask, accept a no, offer alternatives, listen, and protect personal space.
- No forced contact, kissing, romance, manipulation, or unsafe secret.

## Out Of Scope

- No story, challenges, illustrations, or gameplay for Asco.
- No changes to completed island content.
- No mobile polish phase or exhaustive manual walkthrough.
- No changes to the final-island concept for Amor.

## Acceptance

- Completing Sorpresa unlocks Afectividad.
- Afectividad can be opened, read, completed, and followed by its retos.
- Completing Afectividad unlocks Asco as the next prepared map phase.
- The six story images load from project assets.
- Build and available technical checks pass.

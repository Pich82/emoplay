# 009 Confianza Island

## Objective

Make Confianza a playable island after Gratitud, following the existing EMOPLAY island pattern.

## Scope

- Add Confianza to the playable route after Gratitud.
- Provide a six-page story about safe support, clear agreements, small steps, and respectful limits.
- Add story illustrations, final reflection, reward, achievements, challenge set, and related rewards.
- Make Sorpresa the explicitly marked next map phase without developing its content.
- Reserve Amor as the final island of the complete emotional route.

## Out Of Scope

- No story, challenges, illustrations, or gameplay for Sorpresa or Amor.
- No changes to finished island content.
- No mobile polish phase or manual mobile walkthrough.

## Acceptance

- Completing Gratitud unlocks Confianza.
- Confianza can be opened, read, completed, and then its retos can be played.
- Completing Confianza unlocks Sorpresa as a map marker prepared for the next phase.
- Amor remains visible at the end of the map as a special final island.
- Build and available checks pass.

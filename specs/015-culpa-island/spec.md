# 015 Culpa Island

## Objective

Make Culpa a playable island after Celos, following the current EMOPLAY island pattern and preparing Amor as the final special destination.

## Scope

- Add Culpa to the playable route after Celos.
- Provide a six-page story about responsibility, impact, apology, repair, learning, and self-respect.
- Add a character reference, six story illustrations, final reflection, story reward, achievements, challenges, and related rewards.
- Unlock Amor as the next final map phase without implementing its special experience.

## Educational Tone

- Guilt may point to an action that needs attention; it does not define the child's identity.
- The player distinguishes "I did something that caused harm" from "I am bad".
- A useful apology names the action and its impact without excuses.
- Repair is offered, not imposed on the affected person.
- Forgiveness, renewed contact, physical affection, and immediate reconciliation are never required.
- A child is not responsible for regulating adult emotions or repairing an unsafe situation alone.
- Safe adult support is available for complex, frightening, or unequal situations.
- The story avoids public confession, humiliation, punishment, and shame-based language.

## Out Of Scope

- No implementation of the Amor finale.
- No changes to completed island content beyond the progression links from Celos and toward Amor.
- No mobile polish phase or exhaustive manual walkthrough.
- No server, public sharing, or teacher access to private reflections.

## Acceptance

- Completing Celos unlocks Culpa.
- Culpa can be opened, read, completed, and followed by its retos.
- Completing Culpa unlocks Amor as the prepared final destination.
- The story clearly distinguishes responsibility from shame.
- Repair never requires forgiveness or unsafe contact.
- The six story images load from project assets.
- Build and available technical checks pass.

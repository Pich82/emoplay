# 010 Amor Final Island - Saved Concept

## Fundamental Decision

Amor will be the final island of EMOPLAY. It will not be developed as the next ordinary island after Confianza.

## Purpose

Amor will integrate the skills learned across the full emotional route: care, respect, trust, gratitude, listening, boundaries, repair, asking for help, and self-care.

It will focus on safe relationships and self-respect. It will not be framed as romantic love.

## Distinctive Experience

- A living final map where symbols from completed islands illuminate a pink lighthouse.
- A more interactive story with everyday choices and non-punitive responses.
- A toolbox that lets the player choose an emotional skill for each situation.
- A personal closing activity, such as a message to their future self or a small care commitment.
- A final refuge or lighthouse that displays the complete collection of badges and progress.

## Working Title

`El Faro de los Vínculos Seguros`

## Timing

Develop Amor only after the remaining planned emotional islands are complete. The next simpler island after Confianza is Sorpresa.

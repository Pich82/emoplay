# 013 Asco Island

## Objective

Make Asco a playable island after Afectividad, following the current EMOPLAY island pattern.

## Scope

- Add Asco to the playable route after Afectividad.
- Provide a six-page story about body signals, protective distance, adult help, hygiene, preference, and respectful limits.
- Add a character reference, six story illustrations, final reflection, story reward, achievements, challenges, and related rewards.
- Make Culpa the next map phase without developing its content.

## Educational Tone

- Asco is treated as a body signal that may invite a pause, distance, or a safety check.
- A possible risk is checked with adult help; children are not asked to touch or taste uncertain substances.
- Personal preference is distinguished from danger.
- The story never stigmatizes foods, bodies, people, cultures, or ways of living.
- Limits are expressed with calm phrases such as "no, gracias" and never through ridicule or rejection of a person.

## Out Of Scope

- No story, challenges, illustrations, or gameplay for Culpa.
- No changes to completed island content.
- No mobile polish phase or exhaustive manual walkthrough.
- No changes to the final-island concept for Amor.

## Acceptance

- Completing Afectividad unlocks Asco.
- Asco can be opened, read, completed, and followed by its retos.
- Completing Asco unlocks Culpa as the next prepared map phase.
- The six story images load from project assets.
- Build and available technical checks pass.

# 008 Gratitud Island

## Objective

Make Gratitud a playable island after Empatia, following the current EMOPLAY island pattern without redesigning finished islands.

## Scope

- Add Gratitud to the playable unlock route after Empatia.
- Provide a six-page story about noticing help, naming thanks, and returning care without forcing happiness.
- Add story illustrations, story reward, final reflection, achievements, challenge set, and related rewards.
- Let Gratitud completion unlock Confianza as the next map step for a later phase.

## Out Of Scope

- No gameplay or story content for Confianza.
- No changes to existing island stories, challenges, or images except the Empatia reward unlock copy that points to Gratitud.
- No new screen architecture.

## Acceptance

- Gratitud appears as a route island after Empatia.
- Completing Empatia unlocks Gratitud.
- Gratitud can be opened, read, completed, and then its retos can be played.
- Completing Gratitud unlocks Confianza as the next island marker.
- Build and available checks pass.

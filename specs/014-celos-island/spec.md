# 014 Celos Island

## Objective

Make Celos a playable island after Asco, following the current EMOPLAY island pattern.

## Scope

- Add Celos to the playable route after Asco.
- Provide a six-page story about comparison, belonging, facts versus assumptions, asking for attention, and valuing personal progress.
- Add a character reference, six story illustrations, final reflection, story reward, achievements, challenges, and related rewards.
- Make Culpa the next map phase without developing its content.

## Educational Tone

- Jealousy is treated as an emotional signal, not as proof that a child is selfish or bad.
- The player learns to notice body signals and thoughts without acting through ridicule, sabotage, control, or exclusion.
- Facts are distinguished from guesses created by comparison.
- Needs for attention, fairness, belonging, or reassurance can be expressed with clear and respectful requests.
- Another person's success does not reduce the player's value or right to participate.
- The story avoids romance, possession, forced friendship, humiliation, and aggressive rivalry.

## Out Of Scope

- No story, challenges, illustrations, or gameplay for Culpa.
- No changes to completed island content beyond the progression link from Asco.
- No mobile polish phase or exhaustive manual walkthrough.
- No changes to the final-island concept for Amor.

## Acceptance

- Completing Asco unlocks Celos.
- Celos can be opened, read, completed, and followed by its retos.
- Completing Celos unlocks Culpa as the next prepared map phase.
- The six story images load from project assets.
- Build and available technical checks pass.
